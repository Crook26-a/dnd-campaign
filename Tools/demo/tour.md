# Take the tour

This is a small, made-up corner of the world, the Gull Isles, built to show what this app does. Nothing here belongs to anyone's campaign, so poke at everything. If you break it, **☰ More** › **Data** has **Start the demo over**.

Tick each part as you finish it: the run sheet above keeps track, and the 📍 beside a part takes you to its place on the map. The app runs a whole game night this way, with the DM's notes for each scene beside the map.

## The map and the two modes {3 min} {place: kbrind}

The top bar has **Build** and **DM**.

- **Build** is for making the world: drag places around, draw coastlines and streets, paint the inside of a building.
- **DM** is for running the game, laid out for the table:
  - On the left, this script, with your notes and the game log below it. Each box scrolls on its own, and the bar between them sets how tall each is.
  - In the middle, the map.
  - On the right, the card for whatever you click, with the **Action box** below it.
  - **☰ More** in the top bar opens everything else: the Library, Docs, Roll, Where, Party & XP, Notes, Ledger and Data.

Switch to **DM**, then click [[place:kbrind|Brindleport]] on the map. Its card shows the town's notes and a button to go inside it. Every place works the same way. The party is along the top bar: click any of them for their card.

## The players' screen {3 min} {place: kbrind}

Click **Players' screen** in the top bar, then **Open the players' screen**. A second window opens: drag it to a TV or a second monitor and double-click it for full screen.

It shows the players the map, without anything meant only for the DM: no notes, no hit points, no hidden places. In the same panel you can show them a picture or a mission board instead, or freeze it while you look around.

## Into town {4 min} {place: kbrind/pgull}

Click [[place:kbrind|Brindleport]] and press **Enter this place**: the town has its own map, with its streets, harbor and buildings. **▲ the sea chart** in the top bar takes you back out.

Click [[place:kbrind/pgull|The Gilded Gull]]. Its panel lists who you'll meet there: [[npc:ln_bess|Bess Copperpot]], who runs it. Click **Talked** beside her name and type a line to remember; it goes into the game log on the left.

Click **Big sheet** on any person's Library page for their whole character sheet, large.

## Step inside {4 min} {place: kbrind/pgull}

With the Gilded Gull picked, press **Enter this place**. Inside is a battle map: tables, the bar, lamps, the hearth, the stairs. The players' screen shows the same room.

- Click a lamp to put it out or light it, and watch the light change.
- Change the time of day in the top bar (Day, Dusk or dawn, Night). The windows let daylight in; at night only the lamps and the fire light the room.
- Click the chest behind the bar to see what's in it.

Press **Back to the map** when you're done.

## The Action box {4 min}

Below the card on the right, the **Action box** records what someone does outside a fight, credited to whoever does it:

- **Heals someone**: pick the spell, potion or Hit Point Die, press **Roll**, and the hit points go back on (a spell spends its slot, and a higher slot heals more).
- **Makes a check**: pick the skill and the DC. Choose **The whole party** as **Who** to roll for everyone at once.
- **Gives** or **Takes an item**: the item moves from one inventory to the other.
- **Casts a spell**, **Takes a rest**, **Hides**.

Anything else goes in **Something else happened**, which lands in your notes (or the game log too, if you tick it).

## The mission board {4 min} {place: kbrind/pharbor}

Click [[place:kbrind/pharbor|the Harbormaster's Office]]. Its panel has the harbor's **mission board**: each job has a notice the players read, a reward, private notes for the DM, and a status.

- **Show it on the players' screen** puts the board on the TV, as parchment pinned to cork.
- Set a job to **Taken** and the place it sends them to becomes a known route on the sea chart.
- Inside the office, the notice board on the wall opens the same board.

## Set sail {6 min} {place: kgannet}

Go back out to the sea chart and click [[place:kgannet|the Merry Gannet]]. Her panel plans a voyage: pick **Sail to** [[place:kthorn|Thornback Isle]], type the navigator's check, and press **▶ Sail**.

- She sails at the pace you choose, the world clock runs as she goes, and she leaves a dotted trail behind her.
- The navigator's check is a table rule you can use or ignore: a natural 20 halves the time, and a total under 10 adds half a day for each point below 10. Try a total of 2 and watch the trail.
- On the players' screen, the sea is uncharted until the ship comes near: places turn up as she sights them.

## A fight {10 min} {place: kthorn}

Click [[place:kthorn|Thornback Cave]]. Its panel shows who's there (a goblin boss and six goblin warriors), how dangerous that is for this party under the 2024 rules, and what XP it's worth. The demo scales rooms to who walks in (**Data → This campaign**): send only two in and the panel shows fewer goblins; **Start the battle** makes it so.

1. Press **Enter this place**. Going in is always calm: the goblins are on the map where they stand, but nobody is fighting yet, and the Action box is still the one for outside a fight. Talk your way through if you can.
2. Press **Start the battle** when it comes to blows, then **Roll initiative.** The turn order shows on both screens.
3. The **Action box** becomes the fight's own: whoever's turn it is, their attacks and spells, add-ons like Sneak Attack, and **Roll** for the damage (dice doubled on a crit). Click any token or name in the turn order for their card, whoever's turn it is.
4. The cave is dark: only the goblins' campfire lights it. Each character sees only what their eyes can.
5. The chest at the back is locked. Click it: the panel shows how hard the lock is, and a search with a Perception check can turn up what's hidden.

**Next turn** moves the fight along. **End the battle** lays the fallen out to search and leaves the cave calm again, and the place's panel offers the party the XP.

While you paint a map in Build, hold **Space** and drag (or drag with the middle mouse button) to move around it without painting, or switch on **✋ Hand** in the paint panel. On the main map, **H** picks the hand.

## The party {5 min}

The party is along the top bar: click one for their card, with hit points, spell slots, resources, conditions, death saves and rests. **☰ More** › **Party & XP** awards XP. They're built to the 2024 rules from the free SRD 5.2: a fighter, a rogue, a cleric and a wizard.

- **Big sheet** on any of them shows the whole character sheet, large.
- **Short rest** and **Long rest** bring back what each rest brings back.
- Their inventory tracks coins, items and attunement.

## The Library and Roll {5 min}

The **Library** (in **☰ More**) holds every monster, spell and item in the free rules, plus your own. Drag a person or a creature from it straight onto the map.

In **Roll**, **Someone to meet** rolls up a complete person (name, class, level, spells, personality) for when the players talk to someone you didn't plan. **An encounter** builds a fair fight for the party, and the loot tables roll treasure.

## Keeping track {3 min}

The game log on the left keeps the night as you play, and **☰ More** › **Notes** keeps the rest:

- The **game log** writes down what happens as you play: where the party went, who they talked to, voyages, fights, searches, loot. **Start session** at the beginning of a game night and **Download session** at the end gives you the night in order.
- The **ship's log** is the crew's own account, a few lines each session; **Draft from this session** gives the player who writes it a start.

That's the tour. Change anything you like, or **Start the demo over** in **☰ More** › **Data**.
