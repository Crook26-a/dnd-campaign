# Kalatecha City Guide

Oct 7, 2026 · Joseph Crook

## How to use this

Use this guide when the players wander off script in Kalatecha. If they head somewhere new, pick a place, give them a person to talk to, and roll on a table if you need something to happen. Every place and person has a rope back: a detail that points to Lantern Rock or to the captain, so wandering still moves the story forward.

Let them explore for 10 to 15 minutes, then use one of the pull-backs at the end of this guide to steer them to the next scene. Anything they buy for the captain or share with him counts as a gift on the favor track.

## Places

Eight places, the six from the campaign notes plus a tavern and a chandlery.

| Place | What's here | Something to do | Rope back to the plot |
| The Harbor ([[place:kcity/psallow|the Sallowmay]], [[place:kcity/pgreta|Greta's Net Loft]]) | Long piers, cargo cranes, a fish market, and old sailors mending nets. The Sallowmay is tied up at the end of the longest pier. | Watch ships come in, help unload cargo for a few coppers, or listen to Greta Vasquez's stories. | Greta says her grandmother called Lantern Rock's light "a light no human hand ever lit." |
| [[place:kcity/pbell|The Drowned Bell]] (tavern) | A noisy sailors' tavern on the waterfront, run by Marisol Ferro. One wall is covered with the names of ships that never came home. | Eat, swap stories, enter the sea shanty contest, try arm wrestling, or play liar's dice. | The three newest names on the wall are the Heron, the Marigold, and the Silver Tern. All three were lost near Lantern Rock this year. |
| [[place:kcity/padm|The Admiralty]] | The stone sea fortress. Armored guards, the harbormaster's office, and the city's cells. | Look at the ship registry, report a crime, or get arrested. | The registry shows that all three lost ships last logged a course past Lantern Rock. Commodore Quell also warns: "No ship has returned from the Ashen Isles." |
| [[place:kcity/pcharter|The Charter House]] | Headquarters of the Guild of Explorers: maps on every wall and a great half-blank globe. | Talk to Michael Fontaine, buy old maps, or look at the globe. | This is where the Lantern Rock job comes from (Scene 4). |
| [[place:kcity/pcath|The Cathedral]] | The great domed church with the tall bell tower. Sister Amara Bellweather heals here, and Pip Salazar rings the bells. | Get healed, buy potions, or ask for a blessing before the voyage. | Pip swears the bells rang by themselves the morning the Sallowmay's crew notice went up. |
| [[place:kcity/pmarket|The Market Square]] | Fruit and spice stalls, curio sellers, Old Tobias Pell and Admiral, and Madame Zisa's fortune-telling tent. | Shop, have their fortunes told, or chase Admiral. | This is where they find the lantern (Scene 2). Madame Zisa's fortune also points at Lantern Rock. |
| [[place:kcity/pchand|Benedetti's Chandlery]] | A cramped ship's supply shop near the docks, with rope, lanterns, tar, and weapons on the back wall. | Buy gear and weapons. | Dario Benedetti says the Sallowmay has never once come in for repairs, "not in all my thirty years." |
| The Upper Town ([[place:kcity/pmoreau|Moreau Villa]], [[place:kcity/phargrove|Hargrove House]]) | Villas on the terraced hills, with gardens, fountains, and gates. Home of Lady Celestine Moreau and Hargrove House. | Visit a rich sponsor, or snoop around Hargrove House. | Hargrove House's paint is peeling and the garden is overgrown, so Hargrove-Vance is broke. Somehow he still pays his crew. |

## People

Thirteen people the crew might meet. Each has a quick look and voice, what they want, and what they know.

| Who and where | Look and voice | Wants | Knows |
| [[npc:ln_fontaine|Michael Fontaine]], [[place:kcity/pcharter|Charter House]] | Tall, blond, early 30s, ink on his fingers. A former adventurer who is relieved to work behind a desk. | Lantern Rock solved, with no more lost ships on his record. | The commission details (Scene 4). He used to sail with Hargrove-Vance until a voyage went wrong. |
| [[npc:ln_tobias|Old Tobias Pell]] and Admiral, [[place:kcity/ptobias|Market]] | A jumpy little man. Admiral is a one-eyed gull that steals anything shiny. | To get rid of the lantern before trouble finds him. | The lantern came off a wreck on the southern reefs, sold by "people you don't want to meet." |
| [[npc:ln_hargrove|Rupert Hargrove-Vance]], anywhere ([[place:kcity/phargrove|Hargrove House]]) | Silver hair, a monocle, a Guild coat with the badges picked off, a swirl of silver just above his collar, and a journal of the crew's mistakes. | The lantern back, the firebird, and the Guild's respect. | Everything about the wreckers. He'll never admit it. |
| [[npc:ln_marisol|Marisol Ferro]], [[place:kcity/pbell|the Drowned Bell]] | A big laugh and strong arms, and she calls everyone "minnow." | For her wall of lost ships to stop growing. | The names of the lost ships, and that the Heron's captain was a friend of hers. |
| [[npc:ln_quell|Commodore Isadora Quell]], [[place:kcity/padm|the Admiralty]] | Iron-gray braid, clipped speech, and she never sits down. | Order in her harbor. | The ship registry, and the warning about the Ashen Isles. |
| [[npc:ln_okoro|Sergeant Bram Okoro]], City Guard ([[place:kcity/pcustoms|Customs Post]]) | A friendly giant who goes by the book. | A quiet shift. | Hargrove-Vance's deckhands have been starting fights on the docks all week. |
| [[npc:ln_amara|Sister Amara Bellweather]], the Cathedral ([[place:kcity/pinfirm|her infirmary]]) | Gentle, always humming, with flour on her sleeves from baking bread for the poor. | To send the crew off safely. | Heals for a donation, sells potions, and gives blessings before a voyage. |
| [[npc:ln_pip|Pip Salazar]], [[place:kcity/pbelltower|the bell tower]] | Ancient, half-deaf, and shouts everything. | Someone to believe him. | The bells rang by themselves the morning the crew notice went up. |
| [[npc:ln_greta|Greta Vasquez]], [[place:kcity/pgreta|the Harbor]] | Retired bosun with a wooden leg, mending nets. | A good listener. | Old sea sayings, including "never trust a light you didn't light yourself." |
| [[npc:ln_dario|Dario Benedetti]], [[place:kcity/pchand|the Chandlery]] | Fussy, with a measuring tape always around his neck. | Customers who don't haggle. | The Sallowmay has never come in for repairs. |
| [[npc:ln_moreau|Lady Celestine Moreau]], [[place:kcity/pmoreau|Upper Town]] | Glittering jewels, and bored by everything. | Rare and wondrous things for her collection. | She would pay a fortune for "anything that glows." She offers 50 gold for the lantern. |
| [[npc:ln_twins|Nico and Lina]], street kids ([[place:kcity/phideout|Under the Bridge]]) | Ten-year-old twins, barefoot and quick. They finish each other's sentences. | Coins and sweets. | Every alley in the city. They saw Hargrove-Vance's deckhands hiring toughs. |
| [[npc:ln_zisa|Madame Zisa]], [[place:kcity/pzisa|Market]] | Wrapped in scarves, she reads fortunes from tide shells. | A silver piece per fortune. | "You will follow a light that lies. Trust the one that doesn't." |

**If the crew sells the lantern to Lady Moreau,** they lose the flame that leans toward Lantern Rock, but they still have the Guild's chart. Let them choose. It's a good early taste of "free it or sell it."

## Things to buy

For ordinary gear like rope, rations, torches, and weapons, use the equipment prices in the Player's Handbook. Benedetti's Chandlery and the market have all of it. Below are the things only Kalatecha sells.

**The cathedral.** Sister Amara offers:

- Healing for anyone who's hurt, for whatever donation they can afford.
- A Potion of Healing for 50 gold pieces.
- A blessing before the voyage for a 5 gold piece donation. Each blessed character can reroll one d20 during the first voyage.

**The Drowned Bell.**

| Item or game | Cost | What happens |
| Bowl of fish stew and bread | 4 copper | Hot and filling. Marisol talks while they eat. |
| Mug of cider | 2 copper | Cold and sweet. |
| Sea shanty contest | Free | Performance check, DC 12. The winner drinks free all night and hears a rumor. |
| Arm wrestling | 1 silver bet | Athletics contest against a burly sailor. The winner takes the pot. |
| Liar's dice | 1 silver bet | Deception against the other player's Insight. The winner takes the pot. |

**Curios from distant islands.** Roll a d10 or pick one. These are sold in the market square.

| d10 | Curio | Price | What it does |
| 1 | A shell that whispers | 2 gp | It plays the sound of a different sea each day. Useless, and lovely. |
| 2 | Glow-moss in a jar | 5 gp | Gives dim light for a week, then dies if it dries out. |
| 3 | A compass that points to the nearest cat | 3 gp | Exactly what it says. |
| 4 | Tin of never-soggy biscuits | 1 gp | Ten biscuits that stay crisp even soaked in seawater. |
| 5 | A pearl with a tiny storm inside | 25 gp | Shake it and you hear thunder. |
| 6 | Fishbone flute | 2 gp | Fish swim toward the sound. |
| 7 | A mermaid's comb | 10 gp | Dries wet hair instantly. |
| 8 | A message in a sealed bottle | 1 gp | Written in a language no one in the city can read. Save it for a future island. |
| 9 | A carved wooden parrot | 15 gp | Repeats the last thing said near it, once a day. |
| 10 | A map fragment | 5 gp | Part of a coastline no one recognizes. Let the navigator add it to the map as a future island. |

## Loot and rewards

**Rewards for helping someone in the city.** Pick whatever fits:

- A handful of silver, from 2 to 5 pieces.
- A free meal at the Drowned Bell, whenever they're in port.
- A Potion of Healing from Sister Amara.
- A favor owed. The NPC will help them once, later.
- A curio from the d10 table.
- A rumor from the rumor table.

**What's in a stranger's pocket.** Roll a d6 if someone picks a pocket or searches a defeated bandit.

- 2d6 copper pieces.
- A lucky rabbit's foot.
- A love letter, unsent.
- A brass key to something.
- A pair of loaded dice.
- Half a pear.

**If they get caught stealing,** Sergeant Okoro hauls them to the Admiralty. The fine is 5 gold pieces or a night in the cells. Captain Callomay pays the fine without a word, and gives the thief a long look. Keep it light: a lesson, not a punishment.

## Rumors

Roll a d10 when the crew listens at the tavern, on the docks, or in the market. The DM-only column says whether a rumor is true.

| d10 | Rumor | True? |
| 1 | "Lantern Rock's light is a ghost looking for its ship." | False, but close. Something alive is up there. |
| 2 | "Those ships near Lantern Rock aren't sinking. Someone's taking them." | True. The wreckers. |
| 3 | "Hargrove-Vance hasn't had a Guild job in a year, but he's never short of gold." | True. He buys the wreckers' loot. |
| 4 | "No ship has ever come back from the Ashen Isles." | True. Save it for a future island. |
| 5 | "The Sallowmay's been tied up at that pier longer than anyone remembers, and her paint never fades." | True. A clue about the captain. |
| 6 | "A pirate queen rules the southern reefs and wears a crown of shark teeth." | Partly true. There is a pirate queen, Raven Heartgrove. The crown is up to you. |
| 7 | "The cathedral bells ring on their own when someone lucky comes home." | Up to you. Pip believes it. |
| 8 | "There's an island out there that only appears under a full moon." | A future island, if you want it. |
| 9 | "Fontaine and Hargrove-Vance used to sail together, until something went wrong." | True. Neither of them will say what. |
| 10 | "A sea serpent ate the Heron." | False. The wreckers took her. |

## Random city events

Roll a d8 if things go quiet or the players are stuck.

| d8 | Event |
| 1 | Admiral swoops down and steals something shiny from a player. They can chase him across the rooftops with an Acrobatics check (DC 12), or bribe him back with a fish. |
| 2 | A cargo net snaps and a crate of oranges spills down the hill. If the crew helps catch them, the merchant gives them a basket for the voyage. |
| 3 | A pickpocket brushes past a player. A Perception check (DC 13) catches Lina in the act. Caught, she offers to trade what she knows instead. |
| 4 | Guards march a half-drowned sailor to the cathedral. He was fished out of the bay last night and keeps shouting, "Don't follow the light!" |
| 5 | A sea shanty breaks out on the docks. Joining in (Performance, DC 10) earns a free round at the Drowned Bell and a rumor. |
| 6 | Hargrove-Vance's deckhands shoulder past the crew on purpose, looking for a fight. Good for setting up Scene 5. |
| 7 | A goat gets loose in the market and eats a player's hat or map. Catching it is a Dexterity check (DC 12). |
| 8 | A wedding procession crosses the square, and flower petals rain down. Whoever catches the bride's bouquet gets a kiss on the cheek from the bride's grandmother and a blessing for luck. |

## Names for people you make up

Mix and match a first name and a family name when the players talk to someone who isn't on the list.

| First names | Family names |
| Marco, Lucia, Tomas, Ines, Rafa, Bianca, Teodor, Sofia, Emilio, Carmen, Davi, Ysolde, Orsino, Paloma, Benedek | Corrado, Vela, Ruiz, Moretti, Santos, Delacroix, Brandt, Varga, Castell, Amaro, Lisboa, Pereira, Rossi, Duarte, Fennick |

Sailors often go by nicknames instead: Saltbeard, Two-Knives, Lucky, Gull, Patch, Old Barnacle.

## Pull-backs

When it's time to move on, use one of these to bring the crew back to the script:

- A Guild runner finds them: "Master Fontaine is waiting, and he is not patient."
- Nico and Lina run up: "Your captain says the tide turns at dusk!"
- Admiral steals something and flies straight toward wherever the next scene is.
- Hargrove-Vance's deckhands are spotted heading for the Sallowmay.
- The cathedral bells ring the hour, and they remember the ship sails tonight.
