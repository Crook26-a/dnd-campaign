# DM Cheat Sheet: Checks and Things

Oct 7, 2026 · Joseph Crook

The rules you'll reach for most at the table, from the 2024 rules. Keep it next to the screen.

## Calling for a check

- **Only roll when failing would be interesting.** If anyone could do it, or failing changes nothing, just say yes.
- **Pick the ability and skill** from what the player describes doing, then **set a DC** from the table below.
- The player rolls a d20 and adds their modifier. **Meet or beat the DC to succeed.**
- A natural 20 or a natural 1 only means something special on **attack rolls**, not on ability checks.

| **Task difficulty** | **DC** | **Feels like** |
| **Very easy** | 5 | Almost anyone manages it |
| **Easy** | 10 | A little effort or luck |
| **Medium** | 15 | Needs some skill |
| **Hard** | 20 | Only a skilled character, on a good roll |
| **Very hard** | 25 | Exceptional |
| **Nearly impossible** | 30 | Legend-making |

## Advantage and Disadvantage

- **Advantage:** roll two d20s and use the higher. **Disadvantage:** roll two and use the lower.
- If something gives both, they cancel out. Several sources of Advantage still only means two dice.
- **Heroic Inspiration:** a reward you can hand out for great play. The player spends it to reroll any die right after rolling it, and must use the new roll.

## Which skill?

| **Skill** | **Ability** | **Use it for** |
| **Athletics** | Strength | Climbing, swimming, jumping, shoving through |
| **Acrobatics** | Dexterity | Balancing, tumbling, staying on your feet |
| **Sleight of Hand** | Dexterity | Pickpocketing, palming, quick fingers |
| **Stealth** | Dexterity | Sneaking and hiding |
| **Arcana** | Intelligence | Magic, spells, magical creatures |
| **History** | Intelligence | Past events, people, legends |
| **Investigation** | Intelligence | Working things out from clues |
| **Nature** | Intelligence | Plants, animals, weather, terrain |
| **Religion** | Intelligence | Gods, rites, holy symbols |
| **Animal Handling** | Wisdom | Calming or controlling an animal |
| **Insight** | Wisdom | Reading someone: are they lying? |
| **Medicine** | Wisdom | Diagnosing, first aid |
| **Perception** | Wisdom | Noticing things, finding hidden things (like loot on a body) |
| **Survival** | Wisdom | Tracking, finding the way, foraging |
| **Deception** | Charisma | Lying convincingly |
| **Intimidation** | Charisma | Threats and scaring someone |
| **Performance** | Charisma | Music, acting, storytelling |
| **Persuasion** | Charisma | Convincing someone honestly |

- **Searching vs studying:** the Search action is a Wisdom check (Insight, Medicine, Perception or Survival). The Study action is an Intelligence check (Arcana, History, Investigation, Nature or Religion). **Finding a hidden object is Wisdom (Perception)**, as long as they search in the right place.
- **Passive Perception** = 10 + their Perception bonus. Use it when they'd notice something without asking to look.

## Actions on a turn

| **Action** | **What it does** |
| **Attack** | Attack with a weapon or an Unarmed Strike |
| **Dash** | Extra movement equal to their Speed |
| **Disengage** | Their movement doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks |
| **Dodge** | Attacks against them have Disadvantage; their Dex saves have Advantage |
| **Help** | Help someone's ability check or attack roll, or give first aid |
| **Hide** | Dexterity (Stealth) check |
| **Influence** | Charisma or Wisdom check to change a creature's attitude |
| **Magic** | Cast a spell, use a magic item or a magical feature |
| **Ready** | Wait for a trigger they name, then act |
| **Search** | Wisdom (Insight, Medicine, Perception or Survival) check |
| **Study** | Intelligence (Arcana, History, Investigation, Nature or Religion) check |
| **Utilize** | Use a nonmagical object |

## In a fight

- **Opportunity attack:** when a creature they can see leaves their reach, they can use their Reaction for one melee attack, just before it leaves. Disengage avoids it, and so does teleporting or being moved without using their own movement.
- **Critical hit:** a natural 20 on an attack always hits; roll all the damage dice twice and add modifiers once. A natural 1 always misses.
- **Cover:** half cover +2 to AC and Dex saves, three-quarters cover +5, total cover can't be targeted directly.
- **Hiding:** DC 15 Dexterity (Stealth), only while heavily obscured or behind three-quarters or total cover. A hidden creature has the Invisible condition, and gives its position away when it attacks.
- **Can't be seen:** attacking a creature that can't see you is at Advantage.
- **Grapple or shove** (Unarmed Strike): the target makes a Strength or Dexterity save against DC 8 + the attacker's Strength modifier + Proficiency Bonus. Shove pushes 5 feet or knocks Prone.
- **Difficult terrain:** every foot of movement costs 1 extra foot.
- **Influence:** the DC is 15 or the creature's Intelligence score, whichever is higher.

## Dropping to 0 HP

- Monsters die at 0 HP. A character falls Unconscious and starts **death saving throws** at the start of each of their turns: d20, **10 or higher succeeds.**
- Three successes: stable. Three failures: dead. **Natural 20:** back up with 1 HP. **Natural 1:** two failures.
- Taking damage at 0 HP is a failure (two if it's a critical hit). If damage drops them to 0 and the leftover damage equals or beats their HP maximum, they die outright.
- Campaign note: a character's first death is when Captain Callomay appears.

## Conditions

| **Condition** | **Effect** |
| **Blinded** | Can't see; fails checks that need sight. Attacks against it have Advantage, its attacks have Disadvantage. |
| **Charmed** | Can't attack or harm the charmer. The charmer has Advantage on social checks against it. |
| **Deafened** | Can't hear; fails checks that need hearing. |
| **Exhaustion** | d20 tests –2 per level, Speed –5 ft per level. Level 6 is death. A long rest removes one level. |
| **Frightened** | Disadvantage on checks and attacks while it can see the source; can't move closer to it. |
| **Grappled** | Speed 0; Disadvantage attacking anyone but the grappler; can be dragged. Escape: action, Athletics or Acrobatics vs the escape DC. |
| **Incapacitated** | No actions, Bonus Actions or Reactions; can't speak; Concentration ends. |
| **Invisible** | Can't be seen. Attacks against it have Disadvantage, its attacks have Advantage. |
| **Paralyzed** | Incapacitated, Speed 0, fails Str and Dex saves. Attacks have Advantage; hits from within 5 ft are critical. |
| **Petrified** | Turned to stone: Incapacitated, Speed 0, fails Str and Dex saves, Resistance to all damage. |
| **Poisoned** | Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks. |
| **Prone** | Crawl, or spend half its Speed to stand. Its attacks have Disadvantage; attacks from within 5 ft have Advantage, from farther Disadvantage. |
| **Restrained** | Speed 0. Attacks against it have Advantage, its attacks have Disadvantage; Disadvantage on Dex saves. |
| **Stunned** | Incapacitated, fails Str and Dex saves; attacks against it have Advantage. |
| **Unconscious** | Incapacitated and Prone, drops what it holds, fails Str and Dex saves. Attacks have Advantage; hits from within 5 ft are critical. |

## Out of a fight

- **Short rest:** 1 hour. They can spend Hit Point Dice to heal.
- **Long rest:** at least 8 hours. All lost HP and all spent Hit Point Dice come back, and Exhaustion drops one level.
- **Falling:** 1d6 bludgeoning per 10 feet fallen, up to 20d6.
- **Jumping:** long jump up to their Strength score in feet with a 10-foot run-up (half without). High jump 3 + Strength modifier feet with a run-up (half without).
- **Holding breath:** 1 + Constitution modifier minutes (at least 30 seconds).

## Looting in the app

- Nothing on a fallen enemy is found until someone searches. Pick who searches, type in their **Perception** total, and they find everything whose DC it meets.
- Weapons they fought with are in plain sight. Coins and carried things default to DC 10 (easy); change any item's DC on the card or in the Library.
- After the fight, the fallen stay at the place under Loot here, so the party can still search them later.
