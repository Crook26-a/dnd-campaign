**Light, sight and time (2026-10-08):** top bar has "Day N" (root.day), the time of day select (root.timeOfDay: day=Bright, dim=Dusk or dawn=Dim, night=Darkness) and "Next ▸" (day→dusk→night→next day). - 2026-10-28: MAP FORMAT — rooms that scale to who walks in (stage 4; for any builder or hand-made map, Ashgrave included later). All fields optional; a map without them plays as written.
  - Campaign (root): `scaleRooms: { on: true, builtFor: 4 }`. `on` is the switch (off or missing: no room scales). `builtFor` = how many characters the rooms were built for; missing = the party's size (`party`).
  - Room (`node.site`, survey place or plan room): `noScale: true` opts the room out (as written, whoever walks in). `builtFor: N` overrides the campaign's number for this room. The level it was built for is the existing `site.level` (else `node.level`, else 1).
  - Enemy line (each entry of `site.foes`, and of each `site.stages[k].foes`): `scale: "scales" | "fixed" | "with"`.
    - "scales": the count goes up or down (never below 1). Dice counts ("1d4") scale the rolled number.
    - "fixed": always the count written.
    - "with": the line comes only when `scaleWith: N` or more are in the room; otherwise none of it.
    - Missing `scale`: a line of more than one (or dice) scales; a single one is fixed. A library person (`id: "npc:<id>"`) is always fixed, whatever the field says.
  - Hit points: a fixed line's hit points follow by default (×0.5 to ×2, an estimate, not an SRD rule); `scaleHp: false` keeps them as written. Applies to named people too.
