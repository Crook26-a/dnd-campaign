WHAT'S WHERE IN THIS FOLDER

index.html
    The campaign app. Open this one. Leave it where it is.
    Everything you do at the table lives in the app: the party, inventories, attunement,
    coins, the library, what's in every place, the documents.

Kalatecha Map.json
    Claude's newest build of the map, library, party and documents.
    When Claude says it has been updated, bring it in from the app's Data tab with
    "Update from Kalatecha Map.json" (or "Update from a new map file..."). That keeps your
    play and only brings in what Claude changed; where you both changed the same thing,
    yours is kept, and the app lists what came in.
    "Load a survey.json (replaces everything)" is only for starting over from a file.

Players\
    The players' character sheets (PDFs and photos). Claude attaches them to their
    characters in the map.

Saves\
    Your saves. In the Data tab, pick this campaign folder once ("Pick the campaign
    folder"); then "Save to the Saves folder now" writes a dated save here, and the
    autosave option keeps Saves\autosave.json up to date. These are your backups.
    Claude only builds from a save when you ask him to take something from it; at the
    moment that's "2026-10-09 kalatecha (Sallowmay below decks).json" (it has the Charter House edits too).

Pictures\
    The city picture for the table.

Old versions\
    Earlier copies, kept just in case: the Word documents from before they moved into the
    app, earlier map files, and the app's original backup.

Tools\
    What Claude uses to build the map: the builder, the SRD rules data, and the starting
    text of each document. Nothing in here needs opening.
